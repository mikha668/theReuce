Approved clarification — 2026-11-21

- A purchase/order may be registered with zero or more product instances. This
  follows the krav.txt purchase-event rule and takes precedence for the
  first implementation over FR-002's minimum-one-product wording.

Approved clarification — 2026-11-24

- An approved initial assignment records the canonical internal party ID on
  the physical product. A reported buyer reference or an alias is not an
  ownership stamp. A saved approval must not change when aliases are edited.
- Startup, login, reads and ordinary partner submissions must not transfer an
  existing ownership stamp. Transfer, return or correction requires a
  separate authorized operation with history.
- A purchase may still have zero order lines and zero product instances.
